# Apple App Store Privacy Nutrition Labels — AURA GAY 18+

## 1. Data Used to Track You
**None**. AURA does not track users across third-party apps and websites for advertising purposes.

---

## 2. Data Linked to the User
The following data types may be collected and linked to the user's identity:

### Contact Info
- **Email Address**: Used for Account Functionality and Developer Communications.
- **Name**: Used for App Functionality.

### User Content
- **Photos or Videos**: Used for App Functionality (profile pictures, Star Videos, chat attachments).
- **Audio Data**: Used for App Functionality (voice messages).
- **Customer Support**: Used for resolving support tickets and moderation appeals.

### Location
- **Precise Location**: Used for App Functionality (calculating distances on Radar and nearby Discover).
- **Coarse Location**: Used for App Functionality (approximate location mode).

### Identifiers
- **User ID**: Used for Account Management and Security.
- **Device ID**: Used for Fraud Prevention and Account Security.

### Purchases
- **Purchase History**: Used for App Functionality (granting and managing AURA Premium VIP entitlements).

### Sensitive Info
- **Sexual Orientation / Interests**: Voluntary user profile details used exclusively for App Functionality and matching filters.

### Diagnostics
- **Crash Data & Performance Data**: Used for Analytics and App Performance improvements.

---

## 3. Mandatory Apple Privacy Policy URLs
- **Privacy Policy**: `https://aura.app/privacy`
- **Terms of Service (EULA)**: `https://aura.app/terms` (incorporates Apple Standard EULA)
- **Account Deletion Link**: In-app under `Settings` → `Account Actions` → `Erase Personal Data & Delete Account (Art. 17)`.
