## 2. UGC Safety & Moderation Flow Demonstration
AURA strictly adheres to Google Play UGC and Child Safety policies:
1. **User Blocking**: On any profile or in chat, tap the shield or three-dots icon and select **"Block User"**. The blocked user is immediately removed from Discover, Radar, and Messages in real time.
2. **User & Content Reporting**: Tap **"Report"** on any profile or message. The report modal offers DSA-compliant categories including Inappropriate Media, Harassment, Hate Speech, and Child Sexual Exploitation & Abuse (CSAE). Reports are dispatched immediately to our 24/7 moderation triage system.
3. **Mandatory 18+ Age Gating**: All new registrations require explicit confirmation of adult status (18+).
4. **Account Deletion**: Navigate to `Settings` → scroll to `Account Actions` → tap `Erase Personal Data & Delete Account (Art. 17)`. Confirmed deletion permanently erases all personal data, photos, chats, and records immediately.
