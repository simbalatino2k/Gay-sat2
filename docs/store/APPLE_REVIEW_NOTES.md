## 2. Guideline 1.2 — User-Generated Content (UGC) Compliance
AURA complies fully with App Store Review Guideline 1.2:
1. **EULA & Terms**: Users must accept the Terms of Use explicitly stating that objectionable content, harassment, hate speech, or abuse will not be tolerated.
2. **Real-Time Blocking**: Reviewers can block any user from their profile card or within chat. Blocked users are immediately hidden from Discover, Radar, and Messages.
3. **Immediate Content & User Reporting**: Reviewers can report any profile, message, or photo via the "Report" button with distinct categories (Harassment, Inappropriate Content, Spam, Child Safety). Reports are routed to a 24/7 human moderation queue.
4. **Immediate Removal of Offensive Content**: Moderation actions result in immediate content removal and user suspension.
5. **Contact Mechanism**: In-app support and safety email: `safety@aura.app`.

## 3. Guideline 3.1.1 — In-App Purchase Compliance
- All digital features (AURA Premium VIP Pass) use StoreKit 2 in-app purchases.
- Store products:
  - `aura.premium.monthly` (Auto-renewable subscription)
  - `aura.premium.3month` (Auto-renewable subscription)
  - `aura.premium.yearly` (Auto-renewable subscription)
- Pricing is dynamically loaded from StoreKit.
- Restore Purchases button is present on both the Paywall Modal and in Settings.
- Deep link to manage subscriptions in App Store Account settings is provided.
- Free features (blocking, reporting, account deletion, profile editing) are 100% free and never paywalled.

---

## 4. Guideline 5.1.1(v) — Account Deletion
AURA supports complete in-app account deletion:
- Navigate to `Settings` → scroll down to `Account Actions` → tap `Erase Personal Data & Delete Account (Art. 17)`.
- Confirming erasure permanently deletes the user account, messages, media files, and active session tokens.
- Clear disclosure is provided advising users that active Apple subscriptions must be cancelled in their Apple ID settings.
