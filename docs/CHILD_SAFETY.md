# AURA GAY 18+ — Child Safety & CSAE Zero-Tolerance Policy

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Statement
AURA operates as a strictly adult-only (18+) connection service designed exclusively for gay, bisexual, trans, and queer adult men. AURA maintains an uncompromising, zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), grooming, solicitation of minors, and any involvement of individuals under the age of 18.

Any user attempting to upload, transmit, solicit, encourage, or facilitate CSAM or CSAE will be subject to immediate and permanent account termination, permanent IP and device banning, preservation of evidence, and mandatory reporting to law enforcement authorities and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), in full compliance with European Union directives and international child protection statutes.

---

## 2. Age Gating & Onboarding Verification
1. **Mandatory 18+ Verification**: All users must confirm that they are at least 18 years old during registration and age-consent onboarding before access is granted.
2. **Explicit Terms & Verification**: Any account suspected of belonging to an individual under 18 is immediately suspended and placed under administrative review. Failure to provide legal adult age verification results in permanent account deletion.
3. **No Targeting or Marketing to Minors**: AURA metadata, marketing assets, Google Play Store classifications, and Apple App Store age ratings are strictly set to **18+ / 17+ Mature (Frequent/Intense Sexual Content, Alcohol/Tobacco/Drug Reference)**.

---

## 3. In-App Reporting & Rapid Response
- Users can report profiles, chat messages, or media immediately via the native **Report** action present on all profile cards, radar markers, and message views.
- The report category **"Child Sexual Exploitation & Abuse (CSAE)"** triggers top-priority, urgent triage in the internal moderation queue.
- Our dedicated trust and safety team reviews prioritized reports 24/7.
- Direct reporting contact for authorities and users: **`safety@aura.app`** / **`dsa@aura.app`**.

---

## 4. Preservation & Law Enforcement Cooperation
When CSAM/CSAE is detected or reported:
- The content is immediately isolated and prevented from further distribution or viewing.
- The user account is immediately banned and suspended from session creation.
- Evidentiary logs (cryptographic hashes, IP addresses, timestamped records) are preserved in an encrypted, secure evidentiary vault strictly for legal disclosure to authorized law enforcement agencies.
- Reports are proactively filed with NCMEC and relevant regional cyber-crime units.

---

## 5. DSA & Regulatory Compliance
AURA complies with Article 16, 17, and 18 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act) regarding illegal content notifications and cooperation with Member State judicial and administrative authorities.
